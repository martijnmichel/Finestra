Project overview:
Built a cross-platform music theory learning product for musicians and students who want structured practice — not just reference charts. The product teaches intervals, chords, scales, keys, and progressions through guided exercises on iOS and Android, backed by a multi-locale marketing site and educational content. Main features include step-by-step practice flows, interactive theory tools (piano / guitar / notation), localized content, and a broader product suite that grew from the same codebase (practice apps and school tooling). As founder and sole/core developer, owned product direction, architecture, native apps, web, backend, and releases end to end.
